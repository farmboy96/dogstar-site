Dogstar Bookkeeping

The AP Engine: invoices in, reconciled, reviewed.

Accounts payable without the data entry. Invoices arrive by email. The engine reads each one and drafts the entry. A controller reviews it. Nothing posts to your books until a person approves it.

See how it works

In production for one client

The case

Most small organizations pay their bills the same way: someone opens the email, reads the invoice, keys it into the accounting system, and hopes nothing was missed. The AP Engine turns that into a review queue. The reading and keying are done for you. The judgment stays with a controller.
