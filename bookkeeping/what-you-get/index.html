What you get

A review queue instead of data entry
Every invoice arrives already read, with its amounts and proposed coding filled in. The work left is to check and approve.
A reviewer, not a robot
A controller approves every entry. The software prepares; it does not decide.
Clean records
Each posted bill carries its source document, so the paper trail is in the ledger, not in a mailbox.
Vendor control
Unknown vendors are held until a person decides what they are. Nothing creates a vendor record on its own.
Coming: a monthly reconciliation summary
A summary of the month's payables, delivered by email. In the works, not yet part of the service.
