How it works

Four steps. Software does the first two. People do the last two.

  1. Invoices arrive in a dedicated mailbox

    Your vendors send to one address. Every attachment is queued automatically, including a photographed receipt or a receipt sent as an email with no attachment at all.

  2. The engine reads each document

    It extracts the vendor, the date, the amounts and the line items, and proposes the account coding. Known vendors are matched to your vendor list. An unknown vendor is held for a person to decide; the engine never creates one on its own.

  3. A controller reviews every entry

    She checks the coding, corrects what needs it, and approves. What is approved is frozen: if anything about the entry changes after approval, the post stops and the entry comes back for review.

  4. Approved bills are posted, by a person

    Each bill is posted to your accounting system with the source document attached. The posting step is run by a person, never on a schedule.

And then

A monthly reconciliation summary by email is in the works. It is not part of the service yet, and this page will say so until it is.
