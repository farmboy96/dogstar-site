Dogstar Technologies

Set the bearing once. Build by it everywhere.

Dogstar is the guiding star: one bearing that every piece of work is set by. What we build are stars in constellations, one constellation for each line of business.

  • Dogstar: The guiding star. An owner's vision becomes the bearing that every piece of work is set by, and the bearing is kept true as the work grows.
  • Vision: It starts with what the owner wants built, and why.
  • Doctrine: The vision is written down as plain rules that every agent reads before it works.
  • Lanes: Each line of business gets its own lane, so work in one never spills into another.
  • Seats: In every lane, people and agents hold separate seats: one decides, one plans, one builds, one checks.
  • Calibration: Before work starts, every seat takes its bearing again from the same star.
  • Bookkeeping, the AP Engine: Accounts payable as a weekly managed service. In service for a client today, with a person approving every entry before it posts.
  • Healthcare, rural hospitals: Infor CloudSuite for small rural hospitals. A two-hospital proof build is in progress; nothing is in production.
  • Room in the sky: Finance, HR and supply chain: the back office is where Dogstar works, and where the next constellations will be.
