Services

Three kinds of work, all done the same way: prerequisites read first, one action at a time, evidence saved after.

Configuration

Finance and supply chain setup on Infor CloudSuite: enterprise groups, companies, the chart of accounts, accounting units, fiscal periods, vendors, items, requisitions, purchase orders, invoice matching, payment holds and period-close controls. Each item is written as a checklist step with its prerequisites, the one action it takes, and the read-back that proves it.

Conversion

Opening balances and master data brought across with a balance check at the same period, basis and cutoff. The arithmetic is proved before anything is loaded, and the tie-out is proved after.

Build runbooks

Step-by-step runbooks that say plainly what is known and what is not. Every step names its prerequisites, performs at most one action, reads its result back, and stops if the result is not what was expected. A limit that has not been proved is recorded as unknown, not assumed.

No product is in production. These are services, delivered by people, with the runbooks as the deliverable.
