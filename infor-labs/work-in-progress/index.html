The synthetic rural build

A configuration design for a two-hospital rural health system, built entirely on synthetic data. It covers the finance and supply chain path from the chart of accounts through purchasing, invoice matching, payment holds and period close.

Where it stands

  • The build checklist has 23 steps. Each names its prerequisites, its one action, and the evidence that proves it.
  • The first step, the chart of accounts, is an offline design. Every later step is a tenant action that waits for its own authorization and its own proof of route, fields and permissions.
  • Seven limits are recorded as unknown until a live system answers them. They are listed, not guessed.
  • Conversion cases carry opening balances for both hospitals that balance by company at the same period, basis and cutoff.

What it is not

It is not a product, and nothing on this page is a claim of results. It is a method being proved on synthetic data before it is offered on real systems.
