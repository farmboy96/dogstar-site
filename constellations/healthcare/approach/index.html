Approach

How the work is done: one standard setup tested before it reaches you, a conversion with checks on both sides, and a service that runs the system afterwards.

One setup, tested first

Every hospital gets the same standard setup for finance, HR, payroll and supply chain: accounting, purchasing, receiving, invoice matching, payroll and close. Each process is tested on synthetic hospital data before it reaches a real hospital.

Conversion

Master data and opening balances are converted with reconciliation checks before and after loading.

Run for you

After go-live, Dogstar runs the system as a managed service. Staff training videos by role and quarterly software updates are included.

Yours to keep

You own your software subscription and your own environment. An exit pack is available at any time, so you can take the system over whenever you choose.
