Approach

Every step is planned before it is taken, taken once, and proved after.

Configuration

Finance and supply chain setup on Infor CloudSuite: companies, the chart of accounts, fiscal periods, vendors, items, purchasing, invoice matching and period close. Each step names what it needs first, takes one action, and reads its result back.

Conversion

Opening balances and master data are brought across with a balance check at the same period and cutoff. The arithmetic is proved before anything is loaded, and the tie-out is proved after.

Saying what is not known

Anything not yet proved on a live system is recorded as unknown, not assumed.

A two-hospital proof build is in progress; nothing is in production.
