How it works

Four steps, every week. Software does the first two. You approve; we post.

  1. Invoices arrive in one mailbox

    Your vendors send to one address. PDF and photo attachments are queued, including a photographed receipt. Some receipts that arrive in the body of an email, with no attachment, are queued too.

  2. The engine reads each document

    It pulls out the vendor, the date, the amounts and the line items, and proposes the account coding. Known vendors are matched to your vendor list. An unknown vendor is held for a person to decide; the engine never creates one.

  3. You approve every entry

    Bills are ready for your review on a set day. You check the coding, change what needs it, and approve; every change is logged. What is approved is frozen. If anything about the entry changes after approval, the post stops, and it does not post until a person approves it again.

  4. We post the approved bills

    Each bill posts to your QuickBooks Online company with its source document attached. If an attachment ever fails, the bill is flagged so it can be added. A person runs the posting step; nothing posts on a schedule.

And then

A monthly reconciliation summary is in the works. It is not part of the service yet, and this page will say so until it is.
