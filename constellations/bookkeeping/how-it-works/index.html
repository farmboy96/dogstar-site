How it works

Four steps. Software does the first two. People do the last two.

  1. Invoices arrive in one mailbox

    Your vendors send to one address. Every attachment is queued, including a photographed receipt or a receipt sent as an email with no attachment.

  2. The engine reads each document

    It pulls out the vendor, the date, the amounts and the line items, and proposes the account coding. Known vendors are matched to your vendor list. An unknown vendor is held for a person to decide; the engine never creates one.

  3. A person approves every entry

    A controller checks the coding, corrects what needs it, and approves. What is approved is frozen. If anything about the entry changes after approval, the post stops and the entry comes back for review.

  4. Approved bills post, run by a person

    Each bill posts to your accounting system with its source document attached. A person runs the posting step; nothing posts on a schedule.

And then

A monthly reconciliation summary is in the works. It is not part of the service yet, and this page will say so until it is.
