What you get

A review queue every week, not month end
Every invoice arrives already read, with its amounts and proposed coding filled in, ready for your review on a set day. The work left is to check and approve.
Your yes on every entry
Nothing posts without your approval, and every change is logged. The software prepares; it does not decide.
Clean records
Each posted bill carries its source document, so the paper trail is in the ledger, not in a mailbox.
Vendor control
Unknown vendors are held until a person decides what they are. Nothing creates a vendor on its own.
Yours to keep
Your QuickBooks Online company and your data stay yours.
Coming: a monthly reconciliation summary
In the works, not yet part of the service.
