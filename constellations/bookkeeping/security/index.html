Security

Plain statements about where your data goes and who can act on it.

Sign-in
Every reviewer signs in with 2-step verification.
Where your documents live
In your mailbox, your accounting system and Dogstar's systems. To read a document, the engine sends its text to an AI model provider.
A person on every post
Nothing posts to your books on a schedule or by itself. A person runs the posting step after the entry has been approved.
What is approved is what is posted
At approval the entry is frozen and fingerprinted. At posting it is checked against that fingerprint. If anything differs, the post stops.
No client data on this site
We never show a client's documents, figures or names.
