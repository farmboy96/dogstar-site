Security

Plain statements about where your data goes and who can act on it.

Sign-in
Google accounts with 2-step verification.
Where your documents live
In your accounting system and in Dogstar's systems. Nowhere else.
A human gate on every post
Nothing posts to your books on a schedule or by itself. A person runs the posting step after a controller has approved the entry.
What is approved is what is posted
At approval the entry is frozen and fingerprinted. At posting it is checked against that fingerprint. If anything differs, the post stops.
No real data on this site
We never show a client's documents, figures or names. Any example here is synthetic and labelled as such.
