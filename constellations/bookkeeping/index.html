The AP Engine: a star of Dogstar.

Accounts payable as a weekly managed service. Invoices arrive by email, the engine reads each one and drafts the entry, and a person approves every entry before it posts.

See how it works

In service for one client

Why it exists

Most small organizations pay their bills the same way: someone opens the email, reads the invoice, keys it in, and hopes nothing was missed. The AP Engine turns that into a review queue. The reading and keying are done for you. The judgment stays with a person.

It is built by the Dogstar method, and its way of running is the pattern the other constellations follow.
