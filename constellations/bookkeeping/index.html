Small business back office

Your books and bills, handled every week.

You review; we do the rest. For small businesses and organizations of every kind, such as private and faith-based schools and construction companies, that keep their books in QuickBooks Online.

The problem

The books get done after hours in QuickBooks, by a part-time bookkeeper, or by a firm that works from a month of paper at a time. More software only helps if someone has time to run it.

The outcome

Dogstar runs bookkeeping and accounts payable as a weekly managed service: bills are captured, coded and prepared for you, you review and approve, and your books stay current. Unlike a part-time bookkeeper, it does not stop when one person is out. Unlike a traditional firm, it works every week, not at month end. You keep your QuickBooks and your data.

  • You approve, we post

  • Yours to keep

    Your QuickBooks Online company and your data stay yours.
