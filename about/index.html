About Dogstar

Dogstar is a way of building and running software. An owner's vision becomes the bearing, the bearing is written down as plain rules, and every piece of work is set by it and checked against it.

That method is the company. The products are what it builds: stars, grouped into constellations, one for each line of business. Bookkeeping is the first constellation in service. Healthcare is the second, and its proof is being prepared.

We work in the back office: finance, HR and supply chain. That is where the next constellations will be.
