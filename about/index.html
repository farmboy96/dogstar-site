About Dogstar

Dogstar Technologies is run by Robert and Courtney Grogan.

Robert Grogan

Robert has spent 30 years implementing ERP systems. He designs the software and the automation behind both practices: the engine that prepares accounts payable for review, and the build method behind Infor Labs.

Courtney Grogan

Courtney delivers the bookkeeping service. She is the controller who reviews, codes and approves what the engine prepares, and who signs off before anything posts to a client's books.

Why two practices

Both come from the same habit: write the method down, let software do the repetitive part, and keep a person on every decision that matters. The bookkeeping practice is in production. The Infor practice is a method and a build in progress.
