About Dogstar

Dogstar Technologies is a documented, governed way of building and running back-office systems. What it produces is consistent, documented, checked work.

Two constellations so far. Healthcare: a back office for rural and community hospitals, set up to a standard and run as a managed service. Bookkeeping: accounts payable as a managed service, in service for one client today.

We work in the back office: finance, HR, payroll and supply chain.

Who runs it

Robert Grogan has implemented Lawson and Infor ERP since 1997, most of it in healthcare, from rural and community hospitals to multi-hospital systems. He has led finance, supply chain, HR and payroll go-lives and conversions of up to 100,000 employees, and today leads enterprise applications for a regional health system. PMP, Six Sigma Black Belt.

Certifications: PMP; Six Sigma Black Belt; Lawson HCM; Lawson Talent Management; Lawson Absence Management. Education: BS Management Information Systems and MS Telecommunications Management, Oklahoma State University.

Courtney Grogan is a controller, and reviews and approves every bookkeeping entry.
